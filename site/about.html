About CCG Blocks

CCG Blocks is the block-coding editor used in the CCG AI Programme. It is a modified version of the open-source Scratch 3 editor, with an added extension that runs image and sound models trained in Google's Teachable Machine.

Source code

The complete source code for this editor, including every CCG change, is available at github.com/CambridgeConsultancyGroup/ccg-blocks under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.

Your projects and your data

  • Projects are saved to your own computer with File, then Save to your computer. Nothing is stored on CCG servers.
  • Camera and microphone are used only in your browser while the editor is open.
  • Teachable Machine models load from the link you paste. Training images and recordings are never sent to CCG.
